## Lesson 13 Focus

Lesson 13 introduces expressions for:

* Wanting something: **N が ほしいです**
* Wanting to do something: **Vたいです**
* Asking what someone wants
* Talking about destinations and activities
* Using **なにか** and **どこか**

> Note: Vocabulary can vary slightly by Minna no Nihongo edition. This file is organized as a practical Lesson 13 study reference.
