# Minna no Nihongo — Lesson 16 Grammar

## 1. Connecting Actions: ～て、～

**Structure:**

`Verb て-form + Verb`

Use the て-form to connect actions in sequence.

**Examples:**

- 朝ご飯を食べて、学校へ行きます。  
  *Asagohan o tabete, gakkou e ikimasu.*  
  I eat breakfast and then go to school.

- スーパーへ行って、パンを買います。  
  *Suupaa e itte, pan o kaimasu.*  
  I go to the supermarket and buy bread.

## 2. Describing Two Adjectives Together

### い-adjectives

Change the final **い** to **くて**.

**Example:**

- この町は大きくて、にぎやかです。  
  *Kono machi wa ookikute, nigiyaka desu.*  
  This town is big and lively.

### な-adjectives and nouns

Use **で** to connect them.

**Examples:**

- この町は静かで、きれいです。  
  *Kono machi wa shizuka de, kirei desu.*  
  This town is quiet and beautiful.

- 田中さんは先生で、日本人です。  
  *Tanaka-san wa sensei de, nihonjin desu.*  
  Mr. Tanaka is a teacher and Japanese.

## 3. Describing Nouns with Adjectives

You can combine adjectives to describe a person or thing.

**Examples:**

- あの人は背が高くて、髪が長いです。  
  *Ano hito wa se ga takakute, kami ga nagai desu.*  
  That person is tall and has long hair.

- このレストランは安くて、おいしいです。  
  *Kono resutoran wa yasukute, oishii desu.*  
  This restaurant is inexpensive and delicious.

## 4. Saying How to Do Something: ～は～が～

This pattern describes a characteristic of a person or thing.

**Examples:**

- ミラーさんは背が高いです。  
  *Miraa-san wa se ga takai desu.*  
  Mr. Miller is tall.

- 象は鼻が長いです。  
  *Zou wa hana ga nagai desu.*  
  Elephants have long trunks.

## 5. Comparing Similarities and Differences

Lesson 16 also practices describing people and places by combining information about their characteristics.

**Examples:**

- 大阪はにぎやかで、おもしろい町です。  
  *Oosaka wa nigiyaka de, omoshiroi machi desu.*  
  Osaka is a lively and interesting city.

- このかばんは軽くて、便利です。  
  *Kono kaban wa karukute, benri desu.*  
  This bag is light and convenient.

## 6. Quick Grammar Revision

| Pattern | Use |
|---|---|
| Verb て-form + Verb | Connect actions in sequence |
| い-adjective → くて | Connect い-adjectives |
| な-adjective + で | Connect な-adjectives |
| Noun + で | Connect nouns |
