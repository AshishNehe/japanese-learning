## 5. Lesson 14 Focus

Lesson 14 introduces the **て-form of verbs** and how to use it for requests and instructions.

* ～てください — Please do something.
* ～ています — Be doing something / an ongoing action.
* ～ましょうか — Shall I do something for you?
* ～て、～ — Connect actions using the て-form.

## Review

Practice reading each word aloud, recalling its meaning, and using it in a simple sentence. Review the verb て-form separately as part of the lesson's grammar study.
