# Minna no Nihongo Lesson 11 – Grammar

## 1. Counters

Japanese uses different counters depending on what is being counted.

Examples:

* ひとつ — one
* ふたつ — two
* みっつ — three
* よっつ — four
* いつつ — five
* むっつ — six
* ななつ — seven
* やっつ — eight
* ここのつ — nine
* とお — ten

### Example

りんごを みっつ ください。
Please give me three apples.

---

## 2. ～人（にん） – Counting People

The counter **～人（にん）** is used for counting people.

* ひとり — one person
* ふたり — two people
* さんにん — three people
* よにん — four people

### Example

きょうしつに がくせいが さんにん います。
There are three students in the classroom.

---

## 3. ～台（だい） – Machines and Vehicles

**～台（だい）** is used for counting machines, cars, computers, and similar objects.

### Example

くるまが にだい あります。
There are two cars.

---

## 4. ～枚（まい） – Flat Objects

**～枚（まい）** is used for flat and thin objects such as paper, tickets, and shirts.

### Example

きってを ごまい かいました。
I bought five stamps.

---

## 5. ～回（かい） – Number of Times

**～回（かい）** is used to express how many times something happens.

### Example

いっしゅうかんに さんかい べんきょうします。
I study three times a week.

---

## 6. Duration of Time

Japanese uses counters such as:

* ～時間（じかん） — hours
* ～週間（しゅうかん） — weeks
* ～か月（かげつ） — months
* ～年（ねん） — years

### Example

にほんごを さんじかん べんきょうしました。
I studied Japanese for three hours.

---

## 7. どのくらい – How Long / How Much

**どのくらい** is used to ask about duration or approximate quantity.

### Example

にほんごを どのくらい べんきょうしましたか。
How long have you studied Japanese?

---

## 8. ～ぐらい / ～くらい – Approximately

**～ぐらい** or **～くらい** is used to express an approximate amount or duration.

### Example

にじかんぐらい べんきょうしました。
I studied for about two hours.

---

## 9. だけ – Only

**だけ** is used to mean "only".

### Example

りんごを ふたつだけ たべました。
I ate only two apples.
