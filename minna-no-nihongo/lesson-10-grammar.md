# Lesson 10 — Grammar

## What I Learned

Today I studied the grammar from **Minna no Nihongo Lesson 10**, focusing on the existence and location of people and things.

## 1. N が あります

Used to say that a thing exists or is located somewhere.

**Pattern:**

```text
Place に N が あります。
```

**Example:**

* つくえの うえに ほんが あります。
  There is a book on the desk.

---

## 2. N が います

Used to say that a person or animal exists or is located somewhere.

**Pattern:**

```text
Place に Person / Animal が います。
```

**Example:**

* へやに ねこが います。
  There is a cat in the room.

---

## 3. N は Place に あります

Used to describe where a thing is located.

**Pattern:**

```text
N は Place に あります。
```

**Example:**

* ほんは つくえの うえに あります。
  The book is on the desk.

---

## 4. N は Place に います

Used to describe where a person or animal is located.

**Pattern:**

```text
N は Place に います。
```

**Example:**

* せんせいは きょうしつに います。
  The teacher is in the classroom.

---

## 5. Place に N が ありますか

Used to ask whether something exists or is located somewhere.

**Example:**

* つくえの うえに ほんが ありますか。
  Is there a book on the desk?

**Answer:**

* はい、あります。
  Yes, there is.

* いいえ、ありません。
  No, there isn't.

---

## 6. Place に N が いますか

Used to ask whether a person or animal is somewhere.

**Example:**

* へやに だれが いますか。
  Who is in the room?

**Answer:**

* せんせいが います。
  The teacher is there.

---

## 7. Position Words

Lesson 10 introduces words used to describe the position of people and things.

| Japanese | Meaning       |
| -------- | ------------- |
| うえ       | above / on    |
| した       | below / under |
| まえ       | in front of   |
| うしろ      | behind        |
| みぎ       | right         |
| ひだり      | left          |
| なか       | inside        |
| そと       | outside       |
| となり      | next to       |
| ちかく      | near          |
| あいだ      | between       |

**Example:**

* つくえの うえに ほんが あります。
  There is a book on the desk.

---

## 8. N1 と N2 の あいだ

Used to say that something is between two things.

**Pattern:**

```text
N1 と N2 の あいだ
```

**Example:**

* ぎんこうは ゆうびんきょくと びょういんの あいだに あります。
  The bank is between the post office and the hospital.
