# Hiragana

Hiragana is one of the basic Japanese writing systems. I learned the characters and practiced reading and writing them as part of my Japanese foundation.

## Combination Sounds

I also learned common combinations using small ゃ, ゅ, and ょ.

Examples:

* きゃ — kya
* きゅ — kyu
* きょ — kyo
* しゃ — sha
* しゅ — shu
* しょ — sho
* ちゃ — cha
* ちゅ — chu
* ちょ — cho

## Small っ

The small っ is used to represent a doubled consonant.

Example:

* がっこう — gakkou (school)
