# Katakana

Katakana is mainly used for foreign words, names, places, and some other words in Japanese. I learned the characters and practiced reading and writing them as part of my Japanese foundation.

## Combination Sounds

Common combinations using small ャ, ュ, and ョ:

* キャ — kya
* キュ — kyu
* キョ — kyo
* シャ — sha
* シュ — shu
* ショ — sho
* チャ — cha
* チュ — chu
* チョ — cho

## Small ッ

The small ッ is used to represent a doubled consonant.

Example:

* ベッド — bed

## Common Uses

Katakana is commonly used for:

* Foreign words
* Foreign names
* Places and countries
* Loanwords
* Some technical terms

Examples:

* コーヒー — coffee
* テレビ — television
* インド — India
* コンピューター — computer
